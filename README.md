## 👋 Who I Am

I'm **Sumiran** — an **AI & Backend Developer** passionate about building **Agentic AI systems**, intelligent multi-agent workflows, deep learning models, and responsive applications.

- 🎓 **Mentorship & AI Research:** Conducting 8-week AI Agents mentorship under **DevLabs 2.0** at **IIT Bhilai**.
- 🤖 **Flagship Project:** Architecting **StudentOS** — an all-in-one smart AI platform powered by **LangGraph multi-agent orchestrators**, email processing graphs, and timetable management.
- 💡 **FinTech & Web Apps:** Developing **UdharrDe** and scalable financial & analytical tools (`Transaction-System`, `LostLinks`).
- 🎮 **Game Engineering:** Building interactive games and physics simulations with **Pygame** (`Space-Dodge`, `BALL_TRACKER`, `Sliding-Number-Game`).
- ❄️ **Linux & Ricing:** **Arch Linux & Hyprland User** with custom workflows.

- 🔭 **Currently working on:** StudentOS and UdharrDe
- 🎯 **Main Focus:** Agentic AI Systems, Multi-Agent Orchestration & Machine Learning
- 🌱 **Learning & Exploring:** TensorFlow, ChromaDB RAG Pipelines & Fine-tuning
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Python, TensorFlow, LangGraph, FastAPI, ChromaDB, Pygame, Supabase, SQL, and System Design
